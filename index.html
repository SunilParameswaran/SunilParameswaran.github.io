01 / Featured experience

AskAbhi.

Designing a multilingual AI learning companion for career and employability guidance.

QUEST ALLIANCEData science & AI engineeringIndia · 2024–2026
THE CHALLENGE

Help young people access contextual career and learning guidance across languages, while making responses grounded, personalized and appropriate for the people using the system.

MY ROLE & APPROACH

From an initial RAG proof of concept through to production, I worked on retrieval, behavioral classification, Bayesian learner profiling, evaluation and deployment. I fine-tuned GPT-4.1 nano on domain- and behavior-specific data and developed a Dirichlet-based learner model covering 33 micro-behaviors.

REPORTED RESULTS

The project supported eight Indian languages and 1,000+ concurrent users with zero downtime. My résumé reports 92% context relevance and 71% real-time classification accuracy; the evaluation methodology is not included here.

01 / GRIDFLEX

Planning EV charging around grid capacity

Problem: Fleet operators need to charge vehicles before departure while sharing a limited connection with a building’s changing electricity demand.

Approach: Our team built a prototype that uses OR-Tools CP-SAT to schedule charging and replays the timetable against held-out demand, with an interactive dashboard for reviewing risk.

Result: A decision-support tool for comparing charging plans; examples include synthetic data and recommendations require human review.

02 / SECOND OPINION

Making unusual readings easier to review

Problem: Wastewater operators have many measurements to review; a reading can look unusual for its own history without crossing a fixed threshold.

Approach & contribution: I contributed to a team prototype combining rolling historical baselines, an experimental Isolation Forest and a dashboard that makes the evidence visible.

Result: A research prototype for prioritizing review. Example visuals use synthetic data; flags are prompts for investigation, not diagnoses.

03 / AGENT REASONING

When does reflection help an agent?

Problem: Can explicit planning or self-review help an LLM complete tasks in a partially observable environment?

Approach: Our group compared baseline, planning-memory and bounded self-reflection agents on BabyAI, using Qwen and Gemini across 150 completed episodes.

Finding: More elaborate designs were not always better: planning reduced success for both models. Reflection's observed gain for Gemini was one episode, so the result is exploratory.

View results ↗ View code ↗
04 / LLM FROM SCRATCH

Learning how language models are built

Problem: Explore the path from raw text to a conversational model at a small, manageable scale.

Approach: I trained BPE tokenizers and explored pretraining, reasoning adaptation and instruction fine-tuning of a 12.98M-parameter transformer, building on nanochat.

Finding: Lower language-model loss did not translate into reliable reasoning in the recorded experiments—a useful reminder to evaluate capabilities directly.
